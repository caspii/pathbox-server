# Our Privacy Policy
Pathbox was conceived to use as little personal data as possible. Your data is solely used to track your location and 
make it available to whomever you share the link with. Your location data is not used for any other purpose. 

## Personal data of Pathbox users
Pathbox processes what personal data is provided for the purpose of supplying its services (tracking and sharing
location). Pathbox does not track your IP address.

## How long is data stored for?
Pathbox currently stores data indefinitely. If you want your data to be deleted, please write us an email with 
a link to your personal tracks.

## Where is my data stored?
When the app has internet access, your location is sent to remote servers run by Firebase. Firebase is owned by Google.  

When you use Firebase, Google is generally a data processor under GDPR and processes personal data on your behalf. 


## Google Analytics
Pathbox uses Google Analytics.

The following information is sent to Google Analytics when using Pathbox:

* Device, browser and referrer;
* Unique identifiers;
* Approximate location;
* Usage of the website;
* Requests and responses sent to and from your device.

This data is used for

* Monitoring the performance of the website;
* Improving and optimizing the website and services of Pathbox.
* Social media and distributed content

## Updates to this policy
From time to time, Pathbox will revise this policy, without further notice. Pathbox recommends that you 
regularly check whether this policy has been revised. However, Pathbox will notify users via notifications in 
the app about any major changes that would negatively impact User’s interests.

## Requests or questions
Feel free to contact us if you have any questions regarding your personal data. You can also contact us with requests 
to exercise your rights under European data protection law. Such rights include the rights to access, rectification, 
erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and object. You can contact us at: caspar.wrede@gmail.com.
