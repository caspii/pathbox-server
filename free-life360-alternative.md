## Why switch from Life360?

Life360 is genuinely capable — and for many families that’s the problem. You wanted to know
your daughter got to school or your dad got home, and you ended up with an account, a
subscription prompt, and a feed of upsells.

Pathbox strips it back to the part most people actually wanted: **a private link that shows
where someone is.** No monthly fee. No account. Nothing for the person checking to install —
it opens in any browser.

It won’t give you crash detection or driving scores. It will tell you, for free, that the
people you love are where they should be.
