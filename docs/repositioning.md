# Pathbox — Repositioning

_Last updated: 2026-06-26_

## TL;DR

Stop selling Pathbox as an **"anonymous location tracker."** Reposition it as a
**free GPS tracker for keeping an eye on aging parents and loved ones — using the
phone they already carry, with no monthly fee, no device to buy, and no account.**

- **Old positioning:** "Anonymous location tracking and sharing" / "the simplest GPS tracker."
- **New positioning:** "Know your parents are safe — without a $200 tracker or a monthly bill."
- **Beachhead segment:** worried adult children of independent / early-stage-memory-loss elderly.
- **The wedge:** no device + no subscription + no account. Use the phone they already have.

## Why the old positioning failed

The product led with its *differentiator* (anonymous, no account) instead of the
*job* people hire it for. The differentiator has no search demand:

| Keyword | US volume | KD |
|---|---:|---:|
| anonymous gps tracker | ~10 | – |
| gps tracking without account | ~0 | – |
| share location without app | ~0 | – |

And the obvious head term is unwinnable and mis-intented:

- **"share my location"** (14k US) → page 1 is Apple, Google, Life360, Snapchat
  (DR 93–100). Intent is "how do I do this on my iPhone," not "find a tool."

## Why "worried family → elderly first"

We evaluated three lanes and chose worried-family, elderly-first:

1. **Outdoor route logger** — rejected. Feature arms race vs. Geo Tracker (offline
   maps, GPX, altitude, lap stats); users don't pay.
2. **Worried family** — chosen. Emotional, recurring, monetizable.
3. **Asset / borrowed-car** — niche, parked for later.

Within worried-family, **elderly/dementia beats kids as the entry point**:
kids is bigger but saturated (smartwatches, free Google Family Link) and skews to
dedicated wearables. Elderly is underserved, low-difficulty, and a phone-based
passive logger genuinely fits an independent parent who carries a phone.

## What the SERP tells us

Page 1 for "gps tracker for elderly" is a **wall of hardware**: Jiobit, AngelSense,
Theora Care, Walmart medical-alert pendants, Amazon, alzstore, BrickHouse — plus
buyer's-guide listicles. **No phone-app option ranks.** Every result wants a
$150–250 device + $30–40/month plan. The Reddit/AgingCare threads on the page are
people trying to avoid exactly that. The "no monthly fee" complaint is the opening.

## Competitive landscape

- **Uncompeteable platforms:** Apple Find My, Google Maps location sharing, Google
  Family Link, Life360, Snapchat.
- **Hardware incumbents (elderly):** Jiobit, AngelSense, Theora Care, alzstore,
  BrickHouse — all device + subscription.
- **Indie app peers (proof the niche is winnable):** Geo Tracker (geo-tracker.org,
  DR 33, ranks #1–2 for "free gps tracking app"), GPSWOX, Loccate, Glympse.

## Positioning guardrails

- **Consensual care, not covert surveillance.** Never lean into "track without them
  knowing" framing — it's stalkerware territory, gets apps delisted, and destroys
  trust with the target buyer.
- **Be honest about fit.** A phone app cannot serve someone who won't carry/charge a
  phone (advanced dementia). Say so up front — it prevents refunds and bad reviews,
  and a `/gps-tracker-for-dementia` honest-guide page that sends some people to
  wearables *builds* trust and ranks well.

## Site architecture for the cluster

- `/` — hero on the wedge (targets *gps tracker for elderly* + *no monthly fee*)
- `/gps-tracker-for-elderly-no-monthly-fee` — doubles down on the KD-1 term
- `/gps-tracker-for-dementia` — honest guide: when a phone app works vs. a wearable
- `/free-life360-alternative` — comparison/conversion page
- `/gps-tracker-for-kids` — phase 2

## Prerequisites before promoting (product gaps to close)

1. **Self-serve data delete + GPX export** (today it's "email a Gmail to delete" —
   unacceptable for a privacy promise to people tracking a vulnerable parent).
2. **Multi-platform (iOS + Android).** A large share of elderly-parent phones are
   iPhones; Android-only caps the market hard.
3. **Real support address** (not a personal Gmail).
