## Why elderly GPS trackers usually cost so much

Search for a GPS tracker for an elderly parent and you’ll meet the same offer over and
over: a small wearable device for **$130–250**, plus a **monthly plan of $9–48** to keep
its SIM connected. Over a year that’s hundreds of dollars — and a gadget your parent has
to remember to wear and charge.

Pathbox takes a different route. Your parent almost certainly carries a smartphone
already, with a charger they use every night. That phone has GPS built in. Pathbox simply
turns it into a private tracker you can check from your own phone’s browser — so there’s
**no device to buy and no plan to pay**.

## What you get

- **Their current location**, updated as they move.
- **Where they’ve been today**, drawn as a simple route on a map.
- **One private link** to check it all — nothing for you to install.

It won’t do fall detection or two-way calling like a dedicated medical device. It does one
thing: tell you where your parent is, for free, using what they already own.
