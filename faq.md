### What is Pathbox?

A free way to keep an eye on someone you care about — usually an aging parent or a child
with a phone. You install the app on their phone, and you can see where they are, and where
they’ve been today, from any browser.

### How much does it cost?

Nothing for keeping an eye on one person. No device to buy, no subscription, no account.

### When can I get the app?

Pathbox is being rebuilt and isn’t available to download yet. It’s launching soon on both
Android and iPhone — [get launch updates]({{ site.data.site.notify_url }}) and we’ll email you
the moment it’s ready.

### How does it work without an account or password?

When you set it up, the app generates a link containing a long, random key. Only someone
with that exact link can see the tracks. There’s nothing to log into.

### Do I need to install anything on my own phone?

No. You view everything in a browser using your private link. Only the phone being tracked
runs the app.

### Will the person know they’re being tracked?

They should — and Pathbox is built that way. Set it up together. It’s for care between people
who trust each other, not for tracking anyone in secret.

### How private is my link?

The link holds a random string that isn’t listed or guessable, and it isn’t indexed anywhere.
Only the people you share it with can open it. Share it only with people you trust.

### What’s the battery impact?

Small. The app uses your phone’s low-power location services and only records as the person
moves around.

### Can I download or delete my data?

Yes — your data is yours. Email us at [{{ site.data.site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site.support_email }})
with your link and we’ll delete it. Self-serve deletion and GPX export are on the way.

### Will there be an iPhone version?

Yes. The rebuilt Pathbox is coming to both Android and iPhone at launch. Whichever phone your
loved one carries, you’ll be able to view their location in any browser.
